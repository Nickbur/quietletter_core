# Quietletter Core

Quietletter Core is the version of Quietletter that you can run on your own computer or server, so your
digital safety net never depends on anyone else.

Quietletter is a digital safety net: you prepare messages, files and secrets for the people you love,
check in from time to time, and if you ever stop, they receive what you left them after plenty of
warnings. Quietletter Core is that same service in a form that anyone can run at home or on a server
of their own, and the Quietletter app connects to it just as it connects to the regular service.

It exists so that what you set up can outlive any company, including the one that made it. If the
regular Quietletter service ever closes, you, your family or a trusted friend can keep everything
working on a machine of your own. It is free, and anyone can read exactly how it works.

Just like the regular service, it keeps your files and secrets locked: it only ever stores them in
a form it cannot read, and only your recipients, with the passphrase you gave them, can open them.
One extra is available only when you run it yourself: a page that becomes public for the whole
world to see once your safety net goes off.

This repository holds Quietletter Core itself: the small server that people run on their own and
connect their Quietletter app to.

Guides and help: [docs.burakov.net/quietletter](https://docs.burakov.net/quietletter).
